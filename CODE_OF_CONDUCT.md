# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies to every project space (issues, pull requests,
discussions, chat, events) and to anyone representing the project in public.

## In short

We want a community where engineers of every background and level of experience can ask
questions and contribute without being belittled. That means:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the community and for the people who rely on the software.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks,
sexualised language or imagery, publishing someone's private information, and any other
conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report incidents to **conduct@dimensionlab.org**. Reports are read by the DimensionLab maintainers
only, handled confidentially, and acknowledged within three working days. If a maintainer is
the subject of the report they are excluded from handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines: correction, warning,
temporary ban, permanent ban, in proportion to the impact. Decisions can be appealed to the
same address.
